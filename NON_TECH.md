## 2. Record the demo video

- Show the real app working, no mockups.
- Aim for 60 seconds. That's also the Demo Day Drop award format.
- Say what it does, who it's for, and which Gnani models are running.
- Synthetic data only on screen.

### The 60-second script

`demo-guide.mp4` (in the repo folder, not committed) walks through this with
the clicks highlighted.

Before you start:

- Record on the live site, https://ghanti-agent.vercel.app. The top bar should
  say "Gnani speech live". (Locally, set `SPEECH_MODE=gnani` in `api/.env`.)
- Two browser windows: one logged in as Anita Sharma, one (a private window)
  as Hetal Patel.
- In the teacher window, open the menu at the bottom left and click
  "Reset demo data". The weekly call only sends once a day without it.
- One take is about 5 Gnani calls. Do one rehearsal, then the real one.
- The teacher's Chat is voice only now, a big mic with no text box. The guide
  video was made before that, so its Chat screen looks a little different.

| Time | Screen | Do | Say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Login page | Click Anita Sharma | "This is Ghanti. Teachers run the class by talking, and parents hear about their child in their own language." |
| 0:06 | Teacher, Chat | Hold the mic, speak, let go. Two cards appear. | Into the mic: "8B ka physics homework hai lesson 3, examples 1 to 5. Aur Aarav Patel ke liye note: aaj science quiz mein top kiya." |
| 0:20 | Homework tab | Click Homework. Physics is the newest row. | "Gnani Prisma heard my Hinglish, and the agent filed it." |
| 0:24 | Students tab | Click Students. Aarav's card has the new note. | "The note is on Aarav's record too." |
| 0:28 | Top bar | Click Weekly call. Wait for "Sent to 8 parents". | "One click, and every parent gets a call in their own language." |
| 0:33 | Parent window | Click Answer on the banner, then the green Answer. | Stay quiet for 5 seconds while the Gujarati voice plays. Then: "That voice is Gnani Timbre." |
| 0:45 | Call screen | Hold the big mic and ask. The answer comes from the teacher's notes. | Into the mic: "આરવ ક્લાસમાં કેવો છે?" (Aarav class-ma kevo chhe?) |
| 0:55 | Teacher, Inbox | Click End, go back to the teacher window, click Inbox. | "The teacher gets a summary of every call. In a real school this is a phone call; the website stands in for it. Built on Gnani Prisma and Timbre." |

When editing, cut the waits: about 5 seconds after the teacher speaks, 15 after
Weekly call, and a few before each voice starts.
