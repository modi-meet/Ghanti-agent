# NON-TECH

Everything that isn't code. Order matters: post first, then submit.

## 1. Register (do this first, it unlocks the credits)

Form: https://www.gnani.ai/great-indian-ai-internship-registration-form

Fields: full name, phone, primary email, teammate email (skip, solo), stream of
education, college name, how you heard about it. Credits and access arrive by email.

## 2. Record the demo video

- Show the real app working, no mockups.
- Aim for 60 seconds. That's also the Demo Day Drop award format.
- Say what it does, who it's for, and which Gnani models are running.
- Synthetic data only on screen.

## 3. Post it

- LinkedIn, X, or both. One nominated post per platform.
- Explain what was built and the Gnani tech used.
- Tag Gnani AI's official account (LinkedIn: company/gnani-ai, X: @GnaniAi).
- Hashtags: `#GnaniAI` `#GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge`
- Tagging alone isn't an entry. The form in step 4 is.
- Engagement is counted at the deadline, so earlier is better.
- No paid promotion, bots or like-for-like swaps.

## 4. Submit

Form: https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/dfFShbp7W7
Deadline: **15 Nov 2026, 18:00 IST**

Have ready:

- [ ] Demo video
- [ ] Project title
- [ ] The problem it solves
- [ ] Who uses it
- [ ] Gnani models/APIs used
- [ ] My contribution
- [ ] LinkedIn post link
- [ ] X post link
