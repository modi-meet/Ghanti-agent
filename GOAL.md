# GOAL

What winning looks like, and how they actually pick.

## The prizes

- Up to 100 paid six-month internships from 1 Jan 2027, stipend up to ₹1 lakh/month (a ceiling, not a promise).
- Ten awards of ₹50,000 each. One award max per person. Awards and internships are judged separately.

## How internships are decided

1. **Engagement check**: reactions, comments and reshares on the nominated post(s), counted at the deadline.
2. **Eligibility check**: real use of Gnani tech, rules followed.
3. **Shortlist**: eligible projects with the strongest verified engagement.
4. **Interview**: individual.
5. **Written offer**: based on skills, interview and role fit.

So the post gets us the interview, and the project (plus being able to explain
every line of it) gets us the offer. Both matter. Bought or swapped engagement
is a disqualification.

Possible with a Kannada mode: Main Character. Needs noisy-audio testing: Noise Canceller.

## What "good" means for this build

- The voice loop works live, first try, on a phone.
- A stranger gets it in the first 10 seconds of the video.
- Gnani's models are doing the visible work, not sitting in a corner.
- Small enough to finish and deploy before 15 Nov, 18:00 IST.
